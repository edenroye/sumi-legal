sumi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

What we collect

Your flashcard data (decks, cards, review history) is stored locally on your device. We do not collect personal data, analytics, or usage information.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, email, location, contacts, health data, or financial information. We do not use advertising SDKs or analytics SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data storage

All data is stored locally on your device in localStorage. No data is transmitted to external servers.

Data deletion

You can delete all local data from Settings → Clear all data.

Third-party services

sumi does not use any third-party services that collect user data.

Children

sumi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
