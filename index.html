sumi — Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 2026

What we collect

If you create an account, we store your email address and encrypted password via Firebase Authentication. Your flashcard data (decks, cards, review history) is stored locally on your device. If you enable cloud backup (sumi pro), your data is stored in Google Firebase Firestore.

We use Firebase Analytics which may collect device identifiers, app usage data, and crash reports to help us improve the app. This data is anonymised and not linked to your identity.

What we don't collect

We do not collect your name, location, contacts, health data, or financial information. We do not use advertising SDKs. We do not sell or share your personal data with third parties.

Data storage

Local data is stored in your device's localStorage and IndexedDB. Cloud data is stored in Firebase Firestore (Google Cloud). All data is transmitted over HTTPS.

Data deletion

You can delete all local data from Settings → Clear all data. To delete your cloud account and associated data, email us at the address below.

Third-party services

  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — account management
  • Firebase Firestore (Google) — cloud backup storage
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymised usage analytics

See Firebase Privacy Policy.

Children

sumi is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
